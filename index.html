Interaction Guide
How to navigate the reef

Navigation

  • Drag (left mouse button) to grab the seabed and drift through the reef.
  • Right-drag, or hold Shift and drag, to turn the view.
  • Scroll to zoom in and out. WASD / arrow keys also move the camera.
  • On touch screens: one finger moves, two fingers pinch to zoom and slide to turn.
  • Double-click a coral cluster to dive to it. The Body panel (top right) then shows that day's seven readings — the numbers that grew those corals.
  • Double-click the same cluster again, or press Go back to the garden in the panel, to rise back to eye level where you were.
  • Double-click open water to return to the shrine.

Day & Night

The garden follows your local clock: daylight from 07:00 to 19:00, night from 19:00 to 07:00. The ☾ NIGHT MODE / ☀ DAY MODE button switches by hand until the clock next crosses dawn or dusk.

The Hokora Shrine

Click the Hokora at the downstream end of the river to draw today's omikuji — a daily fortune poem woven from your biometrics. One reading per day; revisiting the shrine shows the same poem.

The Jellyfish Lantern

At night a small jellyfish lantern glows on the sand beside the shrine. Click it to receive tonight's whisper: a gentle sleep guide and a short deep-sea bedtime tale, both written from the day you have just lived, from waking until now: how you woke (last night's sleep, deep sleep, HRV, resting heart rate, temperature and breath) and how the day moved (activity, steps, active and sedentary time, stress and restorative calm). The guide reads that day, and the tale's mood quietly mirrors it.

The Drifting Bottles

A bottle rests beside each day's coral cluster. Click today's glowing bottle to write a short message. Past bottles are read-only — the body has already sealed them. Today's bottle can be re-opened within the day to add new thoughts; once midnight passes, it seals.

Cluster Size

The overall size of each day's cluster is driven by the Oura Readiness score (50–90 window, mapped to 0.55× – 1.80× scale). Subtle readiness differences produce obvious visual differences — the garden reads as taller on strong days, crouched on tired ones. The substrate rock and the floating date label stay a constant size across all days.

Per-mesh Life Gradient

Inside each coral, base-to-tip meshes get a small hue, lightness, and emissive gradient. Stems stay a little deeper, tips glow a little warmer — so corals read as living organisms rather than painted statues.
